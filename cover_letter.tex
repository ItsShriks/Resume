Dear Ms. Koch,

\vspace{5pt}
Turning emerging robotics into solutions that work reliably in real operations is exactly the kind of work I want to do. As a Master of Science candidate in Autonomous Systems at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg and a Mechatronics engineer by training, I have spent the past years building and testing autonomous mobile robots, robotic arms and computer vision systems on real hardware. I am therefore applying for the \textbf{Trainee Innovations position in the CoE Automation of Operations} within the GROW program.

\vspace{4pt}

My experience maps directly onto the CoE's technology areas:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1.5pt,parsep=0pt,topsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Autonomous Mobile Robots:} I integrated and commissioned ROS 2 / Nav2 navigation stacks on physical mobile robots, including mapping, multi-sensor localization and validation of edge cases, and documented the results in structured QA reports.
    \item \textbf{Robotic Arms \& Handling:} I programmed ABB IRB1320 industrial robots for pick-and-place sequences, benchmarked motion planning for a UR5 arm with MoveIt, and my Bachelor's thesis focused on a waste separation system with a robotic manipulator, which is essentially an automated sorting task.
    \item \textbf{Computer Vision:} I built a person-following framework on the Toyota HSR that reached a \textbf{95\% tracking success rate}, improved 3D point cloud segmentation accuracy by \textbf{15\%}, and delivered OCR-based document validation pipelines at Navilan Solutions.
    \item \textbf{Mobile Manipulation \& Whole-Body Control:} In my Master's thesis I couple visibility-aware MPC with whole-body TSID control. The same methods underpin today's humanoid robots, which gives me a strong basis for evaluating that emerging field.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}

Beyond the technology, I know how to move projects forward. At Sakar Robotics I led a \textbf{10-member engineering team as Scrum Master}, taking autonomous robotic platforms from concept to hardware commissioning. That work taught me to balance technical depth with clear communication, priorities and delivery. Having studied and worked in both India and Germany, I am used to collaborating across cultures, which I see as essential for a CoE that works with divisions and locations worldwide.

\vspace{4pt}

I am fluent in \textbf{English (C2)}, speak \textbf{German at B2 level}, which I continue to improve, and already live in Bonn. % CONFIRM: M.Sc. completion before 1 April 2027
I will complete my Master's degree before the program start and would be glad to begin on \textbf{1 April 2027}. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I can help the CoE find, validate and scale the next generation of automation solutions for DHL Group.
