On the software and protocol side, I am proficient in \textbf{ROS2} (including \texttt{rclcpp}, \texttt{rclpy}, Nav2, and \texttt{tf2}), \textbf{Git}, and \textbf{Docker/Podman}, and I have working knowledge of \textbf{MQTT, OPC UA, REST APIs}, and structured communication interface verification. I am motivated to rapidly develop proficiency in Node-RED to build realistic automation flows for production-process simulation as described in the role, building on my existing experience designing sensor-driver pipelines and ROS2-based communication bridges. I am comfortable working hands-on with hardware multiple times a week and thrive in environments that demand a rigorous, reproducible approach to testing and debugging.
