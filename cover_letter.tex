\textbf{Subject: Application for Working Student (m/f/d) -- Generative Workcell Tooling Support}

\vspace{20pt}

Dear Agile Robots Recruiting Team,

\vspace{10pt}

I am writing to express my interest in the Working Student position for Generative Workcell Tooling Support. As a Master’s student in Autonomous Systems at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg with a solid background in mechatronics and hands-on experience in robot simulation, CAD asset preparation, and Python-based tool development, I am excited about the opportunity to support your team in scaling the generative workcell platform.

\vspace{10pt}

The responsibilities of this role strongly connect with what I enjoy doing and have practiced extensively:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{CAD-to-Simulation Asset Conversion}: With a Bachelor's in Mechatronics and projects involving custom robot design, I am very familiar with CAD modeling (Fusion 360, SolidWorks, CATIA). I have experience simplifying complex physical assemblies into lightweight, simulation-ready meshes and collision geometries, ensuring that simulation setups remain computationally efficient.
    \item \textbf{Robotics Fundamentals \& Simulation}: Through my coursework and projects—such as configuring path planning (MoveIt) and analytical inverse kinematics (KDL) for a 6-DOF UR5 arm—I have developed a solid understanding of robot coordinate frames, transformation matrices, and URDF structures. Additionally, my work with physics engines like Gazebo, Webots, and NVIDIA Isaac Gym has taught me how to configure and validate robot properties in simulation.
    \item \textbf{Tooling \& Data Workflows}: I write Python daily, particularly for handling structured robot data, config files (JSON/YAML), and numeric analysis (NumPy, Pandas). In my role with the b-it bots @Home team, I built Python scripts to analyze and visualize robot reachability and camera visibility for our Toyota HSR platform. Furthermore, I have designed user interfaces—such as a C++/Qt-based desktop app during my assistantship at Navilan—which prepares me to prototype lightweight UIs or web-based visualizations (Plotly, Three.js) for your workcell inspection tools.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{10pt}

I am highly motivated by Agile Robots' mission to bridge the gap between AI and physical robotics. Having a collaborative, international environment where I can contribute to real-world tooling and benchmark sequences while pursuing my studies is exactly the kind of position I am looking for.

\vspace{10pt}

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to the possibility of discussing how my skills in simulation, scripting, and asset preparation can support your engineering team.

\vspace{25pt}

Sincerely,

\vspace{20pt}
