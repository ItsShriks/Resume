Dear Hiring Team,

\vspace{5pt}
I am applying for the Robotics Engineer position at Siemens Energy India. What stands out to me in this role is that it covers the whole life of an industrial robot, from judging whether a process is worth automating to debugging the robot on site. I have tried to work across that same range, first in industry in Pune and now during my M.Sc. in Autonomous Systems at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg in Germany, which I complete in February 2027.

\vspace{4pt}
My first robotics job was with industrial robots. As an intern at SSIG Manufacturing I programmed ABB IRB1320 robots in RAPID and RobotStudio for pick-and-place sequences, calibrated tool center points, connected industrial I/O and PLC monitoring, and worked with SCADA, MES and OPC UA integration and robot fleet management. I then spent two years at Sakar Robotics as Robotics Engineer and Scrum Master of a \textbf{10-member team}. We worked on \textbf{7 autonomous robotic platforms} and delivered 2 of them end-to-end. I was responsible for hardware commissioning and configuration management, designed robot parts in Fusion 360 and Inventor, worked with servo and BLDC motors, drives and gearboxes, and set up the ROS / ROS~2 navigation and sensor integration with LiDAR, encoders and IMUs.

\vspace{4pt}
Most of my recent work is integration and testing on real robots. I wrote the ROS~2 bring-up for a Neobotix MMO-700 mobile manipulator with a UR5e arm, Robotiq gripper, LiDARs, a RealSense camera and an IMU, so the whole stack starts on boot. On an omnidirectional industrial platform I commissioned a Nav2 navigation stack, wrote a custom EKF for sensor fusion and documented edge cases from rosbag logs in QA reports. With the b-it bots RoboCup@Home team I built perception for the Toyota HSR that reaches a \textbf{95\% tracking success rate} and helped us complete the Help Me Carry task at RoboCup German Open 2025.

\vspace{4pt}
On the manipulation side, my Master's thesis couples a visibility-aware MPC planner with a TSID controller so a mobile robot can manipulate while its base moves, written in C++17 on ROS~2. Earlier I designed and analysed a 3-DOF picking manipulator in SolidWorks and CATIA, and I have published on robotic arm design in IEEE (a strawberry-picking industrial arm). My Bachelor's thesis was a waste separation system with a robotic manipulator, and my Master's coursework covered safety standards and risk assessment.

\vspace{4pt}
I can join full-time and on site in India from \textbf{February 2027}, preferably in Pune, where I studied and worked before. My English is fluent (C2). I would be glad to talk about the automation projects your team is planning and where I could contribute.
