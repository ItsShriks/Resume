Dear Hiring Team,

\vspace{10pt}

I am writing to express my strong interest in joining the Video Computer Vision (VCV) group at Apple. Currently pursuing a Master of Science in Autonomous Systems at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg with a deep passion for ML and robotics, I am eager to apply my background in 3D Computer Vision, Machine Learning, and C++ to help push the state of the art in object tracking for visionOS. Apple's commitment to pushing spatial computing boundaries deeply resonates with my research and engineering interests.

\vspace{10pt}

This role aligns perfectly with my qualifications and hands-on experience in complex, multi-language ML and CV systems:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{3D Computer Vision \& ML Training}: As a Research Assistant at HBRS and a core member of the b-it bots @Home team, I have engineered robust 3D object segmentation and multimodal person-following frameworks (achieving a 95\% tracking success rate on mobile platforms). I have hands-on experience developing deep learning perception models in PyTorch, writing custom ML training loops, and utilizing CUDA for accelerated 3D point cloud processing, improving model accuracy by 15\%.
    \item \textbf{Projective 3D Geometry \& Pose Estimation}: My work heavily revolves around projective 3D geometry and coordinate transformations. I have configured analytical inverse kinematics for 6-DOF robotic arms, designed predictive spatial mapping frameworks using large-scale LiDAR, and am highly comfortable working with 6 DoF pose estimation techniques across complex coordinate frames.
    \item \textbf{Software Engineering in C++ \& Python}: I possess solid software engineering skills in both Python and C++. I have developed offline C++/Qt applications that reduced data processing times by 40\%, deployed custom OpenCV preprocessing models, and am fluent in optimizing and maintaining clean, well-tested code in multi-language systems. My experience extends to CAD conversion (meshes, collision geometries), bridging the gap into computer graphics pipelines.
    \item \textbf{Academic Track Record}: I have a demonstrated record of publishing academic research, with four peer-reviewed publications spanning international journals and IEEE conferences in areas related to robotics, simulation, and analysis.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{10pt}

I thrive in fast-paced environments where I can discover solutions to unsolved challenges. The opportunity to work on object tracking for visionOS and shape products that will delight millions is incredibly exciting to me. I work autonomously, collaborate effectively across teams, and am fluent in English (C2).

\vspace{10pt}

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to the opportunity of discussing how my 3D computer vision and machine learning background can contribute to the VCV team at Apple.
