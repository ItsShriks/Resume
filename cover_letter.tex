\vspace{5pt}
I am a robotics and software engineer finishing my M.Sc. in Autonomous Systems at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg (graduating February 2027), and I would like to apply for the \clrole{} position. Over the past four years I have worked on robot perception, navigation, motion control and machine learning, and the part I enjoy most is taking an idea from simulation to a robot that works reliably on real hardware.

\vspace{4pt}
My largest project is Steve, an autonomous butler robot at the Humanoid Robots Lab of the University of Bonn: a Neobotix MMO-700 omnidirectional base with a UR5e arm, a RealSense camera on a pan-tilt unit and two SICK LiDARs. I am the main contributor to its ROS~2 stack, from hands-off hardware bring-up and safety stop to MoveIt~2 manipulation, Nav2 and grasping with interchangeable grippers and dexterous hands. For it I wrote a 9-DOF whole-body controller as a strict hierarchical QP with joint, velocity and collision constraints. In a kinematic check it tracks the end effector with \textbf{0.01\,cm RMS} error while the base moves (0.55\,cm for a weighted single QP), solving in 10--13\,ms, and the same controller runs in MuJoCo, Gazebo and on the real robot (\href{https://github.com/ItsShriks/steve_wbc}{github.com/ItsShriks/steve\_wbc}). My master thesis continues this direction by coupling a visibility-aware MPC planner with a TSID controller for continuous mobile manipulation.

\vspace{4pt}
On the perception side, I am a core member of the b-it bots RoboCup@Home team, where I built person-following on the Toyota HSR with a \textbf{95\% tracking success rate} and pointing-gesture detection through TF transforms that helped us complete the Help Me Carry task at RoboCup German Open 2025. As a research assistant I worked on 3D point cloud segmentation for UAV LiDAR data (JungleLook), registering field annotations to the point clouds with RANSAC and ICP at \textbf{98.8\% inliers}. At Navilan Solutions I built a fully offline OCR and layout-detection pipeline that reaches \textbf{90--95\% accuracy} on low-end CPUs, together with C++/Qt applications, Docker deployment and CI/CD.

\vspace{4pt}
Before my master's, I spent two years at Sakar Robotics in Pune as a robotics engineer and Scrum Master for a 10-member team, contributing to seven autonomous robotic platforms and delivering two of them end-to-end. That taught me to plan work with a team, commission hardware and track down problems where software and hardware meet. I write Python and C++17 every day and work with ROS~2, PyTorch, OpenCV, Open3D and Docker.

\vspace{4pt}
I am available for an internship or working-student position now and for a full-time role from February 2027. I am authorised to work in Germany, my English is fluent (C2) and my German is at B2 level. I would be glad to discuss how I can contribute to your team.
