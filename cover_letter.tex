For my Master's thesis in Autonomous Systems at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg I built a MuJoCo simulation of a mobile manipulator, and I now use it to test the controller I am developing. So when I read that your team in Garching sets up simulation environments, tries out tools like Isaac Sim and MuJoCo, and turns the results into robotics demonstrators, it sounded very close to the work I enjoy most. I would like to apply for the Working Student position in Simulation for Physical AI.

\vspace{4pt}
The simulation models the Neobotix MMO-700 (MPO-700 base, UR5e arm, Robotiq 2F-85 gripper) and runs a full autonomous drive, reach, grasp and lift sequence. On top of it I am building a control architecture that couples a visibility-aware MPC planner, which works on a reduced-order robot model formulated as a weighted QP, with a high-frequency TSID controller, connected through an asynchronous ROS~2 bridge that I write in C++17. The code is public at \href{https://github.com/ItsShriks/porta_ros2_ws}{github.com/ItsShriks/porta\_ros2\_ws}. Simulation was also part of my job before the Master's: at Sakar Robotics I converted Fusion 360 and Inventor assemblies into collision meshes and simulation-ready URDF models for the robots we built.

\vspace{4pt}
You also mention agentic and AI-driven workflows. On a service robot project I connected YOLO, OpenYOLO3D and Mask3D detections to LLM and vision-language models, so the robot can look for objects from a semantic request and plan the task around it. With the b-it bots RoboCup@Home team I write ROS~2 perception code for the Toyota HSR. My person-following framework reaches a \textbf{95\% tracking success rate}, and my pointing-gesture detection helped us complete the Help Me Carry task at RoboCup German Open 2025.

\vspace{4pt}
I mostly program in Python and C++17 and have also worked with C\#. As a research assistant at H-BRS I trained PointNet and PointNet++ for label-free LiDAR segmentation, kept the experiments and evaluation scripts organised and documented the results (\textbf{0.80 validation mIoU}, \textbf{98.8\%} registration inliers). At Sakar Robotics I was Scrum Master of a \textbf{10-member team} that worked on 7 robot platforms, so planning work and reporting progress to others is familiar to me.

I can work 15--20 hours a week. If remote work is possible at the beginning, I can start \textbf{immediately}; on site in Garching I can start in \textbf{January 2027}, when I will have moved to the Munich area. My English is fluent (C2) and my German is at B2 level. I would be happy to talk about how I could help build your simulation environments and demonstrators.
