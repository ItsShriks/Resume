Dear Spleenlab Team,

\vspace{5pt}
Building the AI safety backbone for autonomous systems across all domains -- self-driving cars, UAVs, maritime vessels, and air taxis -- is exactly the challenge I have been engineering toward throughout my M.Sc.\ in Autonomous Systems at H-BRS. Spleenlab's mission of deploying machine learning directly into safety-critical real-world autonomous platforms resonates deeply with my research and engineering work, and I am eager to apply for the Working Student / Intern position in Software Engineering.

\vspace{2pt}

My profile maps directly to your core requirements:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=3pt, parsep=0pt, topsep=2pt]

    \item \textbf{Machine Learning for Perception -- Object Detection, Semantic Segmentation \& Real-Time Inference}:\\
    As a Research Assistant at H-BRS, I developed and optimised PyTorch deep learning models for 3D point cloud \textbf{semantic segmentation} on large-scale aerial (drone-collected) datasets, achieving a \textbf{15\% accuracy improvement}. I deployed these models for GPU-accelerated inference on embedded hardware (CUDA, Jetson) under strict latency constraints -- the same embedded deployment challenges present in safety-critical autonomous systems. At b-it bots @Home, I integrated \textbf{3D object detection} (OpenYOLO3D) and semantic segmentation (Mask3D) pipelines on a mobile robot platform for real-world autonomous scene understanding.

    \item \textbf{Multimodal Sensor Fusion -- Camera, LiDAR, IMU \& SLAM}:\\
    I have hands-on experience integrating and calibrating Camera, LiDAR, and IMU sensor streams across multiple platforms. In my UAV Sensor Integration project (ROS2, C++17), I implemented SLAM and multi-sensor fusion pipelines validated against real drone data. At b-it bots @Home, I built RTAB-Map-based 3D SLAM and spatial mapping frameworks on a Toyota HSR with LiDAR and RGB-D sensors. I have also designed custom \textbf{Extended Kalman Filter (EKF)} nodes (\texttt{robot\_localization}) for multi-sensor state estimation in autonomous navigation stacks.

    \item \textbf{Motion Planning, Autonomous Navigation \& Mission Planning}:\\
    My Master's thesis -- \textit{Coupling Visibility-Aware MPC with TSID for Continuous Mobile Manipulation} -- directly addresses autonomous mission and motion planning for safety-critical robot platforms, combining a model-predictive planner with a high-frequency whole-body controller. Beyond the thesis, I have deployed ROS2 Nav2-based autonomous navigation stacks, frontier exploration, and collision-free trajectory planning on physical mobile robot platforms in dynamic environments.

    \item \textbf{C++, Python, Linux \& Embedded Systems}:\\
    I develop daily in \textbf{Modern C++ (C++17)} and \textbf{Python} under Linux, spanning \texttt{rclcpp}-based ROS2 nodes, real-time sensor processing pipelines, and PyTorch/CUDA model training workflows. I have brought up and commissioned sensor and compute systems on embedded single-board computers (Jetson, RPi) and am comfortable working at the intersection of algorithm development and embedded real-world deployment.

\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}

I am available as a \textbf{Werkstudent} (15--20 hours per week) or for a full internship, with the possibility of a Master's thesis project aligned with Spleenlab's research directions. The opportunity to deploy ML perception solutions into autonomous systems that fly, drive, and navigate safely across all domains is precisely the kind of impact-driven engineering I am seeking. I would welcome the chance to discuss how my experience can contribute to your team.
