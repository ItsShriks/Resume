Dear Automation Team,

\vspace{5pt}
Identifying a production bottleneck on the shop floor, rapidly designing and 3D printing a custom alignment fixture, wiring up sensors to an automated controller, and writing Python scripts to eliminate human error -- this hands-on, pragmatic problem-solving is where I thrive. As an M.Sc.\ Autonomous Systems student at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg (H-BRS) with a B.Tech in Mechatronics Engineering and over four years of practical robotics and automation experience, I am eager to join Quantum-Systems as the second member of your Automation Team to help scale production and increase process stability for your cutting-edge eVTOL sUAS platforms.

\vspace{4pt}

My background maps directly to your mission of building practical automation solutions across hardware, software, and sensors. At Sakar Robotics, I took full ownership of multidisciplinary automation projects from initial concept through shop-floor validation -- integrating sensors (LiDAR, cameras, IMUs), pneumatic actuators, and custom end-effectors onto autonomous mobile platforms. I designed mechanical brackets in CAD, iterated prototypes using 3D printing, diagnosed hardware/software deviations through rigorous root-cause analysis, and coordinated directly with external suppliers and machine builders. Earlier, at SSIG Manufacturing Advancements, I programmed \textbf{industrial robotic arms} using \textbf{ABB RAPID} in a live production environment -- designing automated cell sequences, integrating sensor I/O, and supporting production technicians with hands-on troubleshooting.

\vspace{4pt}

On the software and sensor bring-up side, I develop modular automation scripts and data processing pipelines in \textbf{Python} and \textbf{C++17}. As a Perception \& Navigation Core Member at b-it bots @Home, I built and validated multi-sensor automation systems achieving a \textbf{95\% tracking success rate}. Furthermore, my research at H-BRS processing 3D LiDAR point clouds on aerial drone datasets gave me deep familiarity with UAV-specific sensor suites, calibration workflows, and embedded Linux/GPU hardware. I communicate effectively across multidisciplinary teams in both \textbf{English (C2)} and \textbf{German (B2)}, bridging the gap between production operators, engineers, and external partners.

\vspace{4pt}

I am excited by the opportunity to take end-to-end ownership of automation ideas at Quantum-Systems, developing solutions that directly enhance product quality and production efficiency. I am available as a Working Student (Werkstudent) for up to \textbf{20 hours per week} and can start immediately, with full readiness to work on-site in Gilching (utilizing the company shuttle from Munich). I look forward to discussing how my hands-on mechatronics and automation background can add immediate value to your growing team.
