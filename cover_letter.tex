Dear Hiring Team,

\vspace{5pt}
Deploying mobile robots and mobile manipulators that navigate safely and reliably in dynamic, real-world customer environments requires deep navigation expertise, robust software engineering, and disciplined on-robot commissioning. As an M.Sc.\ Autonomous Systems student at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg (H-BRS) specializing in mobile manipulation control architectures, holding a B.Tech in Mechatronics Engineering and over four years of practical robotics experience, I am excited to apply for the Navigation Software Engineer position at Neura Robotics. I am eager to leverage my expertise in ROS~2, SLAM, software adapters, and navigation stack integration to advance Neura's next-generation autonomous platforms.

\vspace{4pt}

Throughout my academic and industrial career, I have focused on solving core navigation and state estimation challenges on physical robot platforms. As a Core Member of the b-it bots @Home team, I integrated, tuned, and validated ROS~2 navigation pipelines on mobile robots and mobile manipulators, achieving a \textbf{95\% tracking success rate} under demanding conditions. My hands-on commissioning work spans 2D/3D map generation (Cartographer, RTAB-Map), multi-sensor localization tuning (LiDAR, RGB-D, IMU, wheel odometry), trajectory validation, and crafting robust recovery behaviors. In my ongoing Master's thesis, I am developing an asynchronous ROS~2 bridge coupling a visibility-aware Model Predictive Controller (MPC) with a Task-Space Inverse Dynamics (TSID) controller for continuous mobile manipulation, ensuring strict real-time control plane execution.

\vspace{4pt}

On the software engineering and diagnostics side, I build modular, maintainable software adapters and middleware bridges in \textbf{Modern C++ (C++17)} and \textbf{Python}, interfacing navigation engines with lower-level hardware drivers and higher-level RESTful APIs and fleet management concepts (such as VDA5050). When isolating edge-case navigation failures or coordinate frame (\texttt{tf}) drift, I rely on structured root-cause analysis utilizing \textbf{rosbag playback, RViz, Foxglove Studio, and Docker logs}. Earlier, at Sakar Robotics, I led on-site AMR commissioning, hardware-software integration, and QA acceptance testing across diverse operating environments, establishing standard deployment and safety protocols.

\vspace{4pt}

Neura Robotics' vision of cognitive robotics and seamless human-robot collaboration in industrial and service spaces deeply inspires me. I bring a strong collaborative mindset, effective communication in \textbf{English (C2)} and \textbf{German (B2)}, and total enthusiasm for working hands-on with real robot hardware. I am available for a \textbf{Full-Time} position and look forward to discussing how my navigation and software integration experience will contribute to Neura Robotics' mobile platforms.

\vspace{10pt}
