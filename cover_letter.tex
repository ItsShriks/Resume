\textbf{Subject: Application -- Master Thesis: Computer Vision for Motorsport Video Analysis}

\vspace{15pt}

Dear Hiring Team,

\vspace{10pt}

I am writing to apply for the Master's Thesis opportunity on Computer Vision for Motorsport Video Analysis at Toyota Racing. The challenge of extracting structured, actionable insights from complex, high-speed race video data -- dealing with occlusions, lighting variability, and visually similar vehicles -- is exactly the kind of applied computer vision problem I am passionate about and well-equipped to tackle.

I am a Master's student in Autonomous Systems at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg with a B.Tech in Mechatronics Engineering. My research and practical work sit at the intersection of deep learning, object detection, and real-world perception pipelines -- skills that map directly to this thesis:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=3pt, topsep=4pt]

    \item \textbf{Object Detection \& Video Analysis}: As a Research Assistant at H-BRS, I developed and evaluated CUDA-accelerated deep learning models (PyTorch, Open3D) for 3D perception on UAV/drone datasets, improving model accuracy by 15\%. I have hands-on experience designing, training, and benchmarking detection models including YOLO-style architectures -- directly applicable to vehicle detection in WEC race footage.

    \item \textbf{Computer Vision \& Tracking}: As a core member of the b-it bots @Home team, I implemented a multimodal person-following framework using OpenCV, MediaPipe, and ROS2, achieving a 95\% tracking success rate in dynamic, real-world environments. Handling occlusions, camera motion, and appearance similarity in cluttered scenes is a challenge I tackle daily.

    \item \textbf{Robust Perception under Real-World Conditions}: In the \textit{Robot Butler} project, I integrated RTAB-Map SLAM, Open3D-based 3D segmentation (SAM3, Open-YOLO3D), and LLM-driven NLP into a unified autonomous perception system -- experience in building perception pipelines that remain robust across variable lighting and complex environments.

    \item \textbf{Evaluation \& Structured Output}: I am experienced in rigorous model evaluation using objective metrics (mAP, IoU, precision/recall), structured experiment logging, and generating clean, reproducible outputs for downstream analysis -- matching the requirement to generate structured vehicle position data for further race engineering analysis.

    \item \textbf{ML Stack \& Cloud-Ready Pipelines}: My daily stack includes Python (PyTorch, NumPy, Pandas, OpenCV), Git, Docker, and Linux environments. I am comfortable working with large datasets and cloud-based data access workflows (e.g., AWS S3-style pipelines), and I have experience writing scalable data processing scripts for sensor recordings.

\end{itemize}

\vspace{8pt}

What excites me most about this thesis is the opportunity to work on real-world, production-relevant video data from one of the most demanding motorsport championships in the world -- and to contribute improvements that directly feed into professional race engineering decisions. I bring not just the technical depth, but also the analytical mindset and drive to push beyond baseline performance.

\vspace{8pt}

I am available to start \textbf{as soon as possible (ab sofort)} and am fully committed to a minimum six-month engagement. I would be very happy to discuss how my background aligns with your team's goals.
