Dear RoboService Hiring Team,

\vspace{5pt}
Putting humanoid robots onto real European manufacturing lines is exactly the step I want to work on: robots that leave the lab and do useful, reliable work in a factory. I am a robotics engineer finishing my M.Sc. in Autonomous Systems at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg (February 2027), with 4+ years of hands-on robotics, and I would like to apply for the \clrole{} position in your Berlin team.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{ROS~2 on a non-standard platform, from drivers to manipulation.} At the Humanoid Robots Lab of the University of Bonn I am the main developer of the ROS~2 stack for Steve, a custom mobile manipulator: a Neobotix MMO-700 omnidirectional base, a UR5e arm, a Robotiq gripper, a RealSense camera on a Dynamixel pan-tilt unit and two SICK LiDARs. I wrote the hardware bring-up so every driver starts hands-off at power-on, with a drive heartbeat watchdog, LiDARs identified by serial number and a shared safety stop, and integrated Nav2, MoveIt~2 and swap-in end effectors, including the PSYONIC Ability Hand on the real robot. The same code runs in MuJoCo, Gazebo and on the hardware (\href{https://github.com/ItsShriks/steve_ros2_ws}{github.com/ItsShriks/steve\_ros2\_ws}).

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{From simulation to a precise real robot.} Steve has to grasp from a table while its base keeps moving, but with a weighted single QP the arm and base tasks fought each other and the gripper drifted by 0.55\,cm RMS. I wrote a 9-DOF whole-body controller as a strict task hierarchy with the exact Jacobian from the MuJoCo model plus joint, velocity and collision constraints. In the same check the error dropped to \textbf{0.01\,cm RMS} at 10--13\,ms per solve. On the real robot I switched the UR5e to velocity commands, which cut figure-8 tracking error from 0.17 to \textbf{0.12\,cm RMS}, and calibrated the wrist camera without markers to 0.17\,cm RMS. Steve now completes full grasps on the real robot (\href{https://github.com/ItsShriks/steve_wbc}{github.com/ItsShriks/steve\_wbc}).

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{Reliability under competition conditions.} In the b-it bots RoboCup@Home team I built person following and pointing-gesture detection on the Toyota HSR with ROS~2, MediaPipe and TF transforms, reaching a \textbf{95\% tracking success rate}. I calibrated RGB-D, LiDAR and odometry, debugged hardware--software issues with rosbag, RViz and Foxglove, and helped the team complete the Help Me Carry task at RoboCup German Open 2025.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{Industrial roots.} I started on the factory side: programming ABB IRB1320 robots in RAPID with TCP calibration and PLC I/O at SSIG Manufacturing, then two years at Sakar Robotics as robotics engineer and Scrum Master of a 10-member team, commissioning hardware across seven autonomous platforms and delivering two end-to-end. I write Python every day, and C++17 where performance matters.

\vspace{4pt}
I am available full time from January 2027. I am authorised to work in Germany, happy to relocate to Berlin to work in person with your team, and my English is fluent (C2), with German at B2. I would be glad to show you Steve's stack in more detail.
