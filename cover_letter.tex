Dear Neura Robotics Team,

\vspace{5pt}
Building the API-first architecture and real-time sync loops that perfectly mirror physical robots in a virtual ecosystem is precisely the challenge I have been engineering toward throughout my M.Sc.\ in Autonomous Systems at H-BRS. Neura Robotics' vision of the Neuraverse -- a unified environment where robots, developers, and simulation converge -- resonates deeply with my experience in designing distributed ROS2 architectures and symmetric control planes. I am eager to apply for the Integration \& Distributed Systems Engineer position to help bridge the gap between simulation and real-world fleets.

\vspace{2pt}

My technical profile maps directly to your core requirements for the Cognitive Twin:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=3pt, parsep=0pt, topsep=2pt]

    \item \textbf{Transport, Real-Time Sync \& Robotics Middleware}:\\
    I have extensive hands-on experience designing and operating distributed architectures using \textbf{ROS2 Humble} and \textbf{Fast DDS}. At b-it bots @Home, I developed real-time telemetry sync loops and managed coordinate frames (\texttt{tf2}) on mobile platforms, achieving a 95\% tracking success rate in dynamic environments. In my UAV Sensor Integration project (C++17, Python), I built multi-sensor SLAM pipelines validated against real drone telemetry under strict real-time and low-latency constraints.

    \item \textbf{Symmetric Control Planes \& Device-Data Ingestion}:\\
    My Master's thesis -- \textit{Coupling Visibility-Aware MPC with TSID for Continuous Mobile Manipulation} -- directly involves building asynchronous ROS2 bridges and high-frequency control loops that operate identically across virtual simulators (Gazebo) and physical robots. I am highly proficient in establishing solid inter-process communication, calibrating device-data ingestion (Camera, LiDAR, IMU), and hardening prototype control architectures into robust deployment systems.

    \item \textbf{C++17, Python, APIs \& Distributed Robustness}:\\
    I develop daily in \textbf{Modern C++ (C++17)} and \textbf{Python} under Linux, building \texttt{rclcpp}-based nodes, real-time sensor processing pipelines, and containerised deployments (Docker). I have built C++ API architectures for document processing automation at Navilan Solutions, focusing on processing efficiency and system robustness. Furthermore, I am comfortable leveraging modern AI coding tools and integrating them into production-quality workflows.

\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}

I am available for a full-time position and am ready to contribute immediately to the Neuraverse ecosystem. The opportunity to own the distributed communication layer and ensure that commanding a virtual robot and a physical one behaves identically is exactly the impact-driven engineering role I am seeking. I would welcome the chance to discuss how my expertise in distributed robotics software can strengthen your Cognitive Twin architecture.
