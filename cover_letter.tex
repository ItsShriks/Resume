Dear Founding Team,

\vspace{5pt}
Designing a sensor mount that has to survive a factory floor, wiring up a pneumatic gripper to an industrial controller, and then systematically debugging why the robot arm misses its pick position by three millimetres -- this is exactly the kind of end-to-end, hands-on engineering work that excites me most. As an M.Sc.\ Autonomous Systems student at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg (H-BRS) with a B.Tech in Mechatronics Engineering and over four years of practical robotics experience spanning industrial robot programming, sensor integration, and prototype manufacturing, I am excited to join your early-stage team and help bring Robot Learning and Physical AI into real industrial deployments.

\vspace{4pt}

My background maps directly to what you are building. At Sakar Robotics, I designed and deployed ROS/ROS2-based perception and navigation architectures for autonomous mobile robots -- including hands-on hardware bring-up, \textbf{pneumatics assembly}, \textbf{end-effector} configuration, and sensor integration across multiple robot platforms. I manufactured, assembled, and iteratively tested robotic prototypes, documented integration failures with root-cause analysis, and led a 10-member cross-functional team. Earlier, at SSIG Manufacturing, I programmed \textbf{industrial robotic arms} using \textbf{RAPID} -- developing automated application workflows and verifying control loop execution in a live production environment -- which gives me a concrete reference point for the industrial robot programming (UR, Kuka, Fanuc) your team works with. As a Perception \& Navigation Core Member at b-it bots @Home (H-BRS), I further built and validated multi-sensor robot systems in real environments, achieving a \textbf{95\% tracking success rate} and maintaining structured technical documentation for robotic cell configurations.

\vspace{4pt}

On the applied engineering side, I am proficient in \textbf{ROS2}, \textbf{C++17}, \textbf{Python}, \textbf{CAD design}, and \textbf{3D printing}, with hands-on experience integrating cameras, LiDAR, IMUs, and actuators onto real robot hardware. I am pragmatic and solution-oriented -- I enjoy taking complex systems apart, understanding failure modes, and iterating quickly to a working solution. The prospect of contributing across the full robotics stack -- from mechanical design and prototype assembly to sensor integration and on-site customer deployments -- at a startup backed by a leading European VC and rooted in TUM MIRMI's research environment is a compelling match for where I want to grow.

\vspace{4pt}

I am available as a Werkstudent for up to 20 hours per week and can begin immediately. I am on track to complete my M.Sc.\ in Autonomous Systems in \textbf{February 2027}, and I look forward to the opportunity to grow with your team through and beyond graduation. I would be delighted to discuss how my mechatronics and robotics engineering background can contribute directly to your mission.
