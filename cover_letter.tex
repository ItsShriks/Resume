Dear Hiring Team,

\vspace{5pt}
At ZEISS, measurement accuracy is part of the product, and that is why this internship stands out to me. Most of my vision work so far has been about 3D data from real sensors, and the step from ``the model found the object'' to ``we know where it is, and how precisely'' is the part I find most interesting. I am studying for an M.Sc. in Autonomous Systems at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg and would like to apply for the internship on your 3D computer vision research projects.

\vspace{4pt}
The closest match to your topics is JungleLook, which grew out of my research assistant job at H-BRS. I built a label-free segmentation pipeline for UAV LiDAR point clouds: geometric features such as height above ground and eigenvalues create pseudo-labels, which then train PointNet and PointNet++. That reached 0.80 validation mIoU, which I report as preliminary because it is measured against the pseudo-labels and not against real ground truth. The more solid number is the registration step, where I aligned field annotations to the point clouds with RANSAC and ICP at \textbf{98.8\% inliers} and \textbf{0.11\,m RMS}. The code runs on CPU, CUDA and Apple MPS and is public at \href{https://github.com/ItsShriks/JungleLook}{github.com/ItsShriks/JungleLook}.

\vspace{4pt}
For keypoints and pose, my main work is with the b-it bots RoboCup@Home team. On the Toyota HSR I built person-following with MediaPipe and OpenCV that reaches a \textbf{95\% tracking success rate}, and pointing-gesture detection that intersects a ray with the scene through TF transforms between camera, base and arm frames. It helped us complete the Help Me Carry task at RoboCup German Open 2025. I have also built a YOLOv8-based person tracker with pose estimation on RGB-D cameras (RoboCompanion), and on a service robot I combined YOLO, OpenYOLO3D and Mask3D detections with RTAB-Map 3D SLAM.

\vspace{4pt}
Inspection under tight compute limits is something I did at Navilan Solutions, where I built an offline layout-detection and OCR pipeline that reaches \textbf{90--95\% accuracy} on low-end CPUs. For the PadhLO project I generated synthetic invoice images as training data and fine-tuned a pretrained YOLOv11 to detect document fields, which covers two more of your topics (\href{https://github.com/ItsShriks/Padh_Lo}{github.com/ItsShriks/Padh\_Lo}). I write Python and C++17 daily, use PyTorch and Open3D for 3D work, and run most of my robot code on ROS~2. For PrithviVision I annotated my own aerial dataset and compared U-Net, YOLO and Mask R-CNN on it, so building a dataset and benchmarking methods on it is familiar to me.

\vspace{4pt}
I can start immediately and am enrolled until my graduation in February 2027. After that I am looking for a full-time position, so I would be very interested in staying on with the team. My English is fluent (C2) and my German is at B2 level. I would be glad to talk about which of your projects my experience fits best.
