Scaling robotic automation and smart computer vision across global logistics requires moving boldly from emerging concept exploration to robust, operational Proof of Concepts (PoCs). As a Master of Science student in Autonomous Systems at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg (graduating before April 2027) with a Bachelor of Technology in Mechatronics Engineering and over four years of hands-on experience in autonomous mobile robots (AMRs), robotic arm manipulation, and perception pipelines, I am thrilled to apply for the \textbf{Trainee Innovations International -- CoE Automation of Operations} position within DHL Group's GROW Graduate Program.

\vspace{4pt}

My technical and research background directly aligns with the CoE's core technological pillars:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt,parsep=0pt,topsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs) \& Navigation:} As a Core Member of the b-it bots @Home team and previously at Sakar Robotics, I have integrated and commissioned ROS~2 and Nav2 stacks on physical mobile platforms, calibrated LiDAR/camera/IMU sensor arrays, and achieved a \textbf{95\% tracking success rate} while establishing standardized deployment and recovery protocols.
    \item \textbf{Robotic Arm Solutions \& Manipulation:} I possess hands-on industrial experience programming robotic manipulators (ABB RAPID) for automated handling and sorting sequences. In my Master's thesis, I am coupling Model Predictive Control (MPC) with Task-Space Inverse Dynamics (TSID) for continuous mobile manipulation, bridging control theory with physical execution.
    \item \textbf{Computer Vision \& Emerging Technologies:} In applied research at H-BRS, I engineered PyTorch/CUDA 3D point cloud perception pipelines that delivered a \textbf{15\% accuracy improvement}. I am deeply passionate about evaluating cutting-edge computer vision models and emerging humanoid systems for logistics tasks like automated unloading and parcel induction.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}

Beyond technical execution, I understand that innovation in logistics creates impact only when successfully integrated into real-world operations. At Navilan Solutions, I hardened early-stage software prototypes into scalable Dockerized services with structured benchmarking metrics. Furthermore, leading a 10-member cross-functional engineering team as Scrum Master gave me invaluable international project management experience—coordinating across multidisciplinary teams, defining quality acceptance criteria, and communicating complex technical solutions to diverse stakeholders.

\vspace{4pt}

Based right here in Bonn, I am excited by the prospect of contributing globally across DHL Group's divisions during the 18-month GROW program. Fluent in \textbf{English (C2)} and proficient in \textbf{German (B2)}, I bring deep technical curiosity, rigorous problem-solving skills, and a strong collaborative drive. I look forward to the opportunity to discuss how my robotics and innovation background can contribute to shaping the future of automated operations at DHL Group.
