As a Master of Science candidate in Autonomous Systems at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg (H-BRS) and holder of a Bachelor of Technology in Mechatronics Engineering, I have dedicated my career to bridging advanced execution logic, kinematics, and sensor feedback with physical machines. I am thrilled to apply for the \textbf{Software \& Robotics Engineer} role at EVASIVE ROBOTICS to help set the new standard for industrial automation and motion planning.

\vspace{4pt}

My technical background and engineering experience directly align with your mission to take projects end-to-end from software to production hardware:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1.5pt,parsep=0pt,topsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Motion Planning, Kinematics \& Control Loops:} In my Master’s thesis project, I am developing a unified control architecture coupling visibility-aware Model Predictive Control (MPC) with high-frequency Task-Space Inverse Dynamics (TSID) for continuous manipulation, building asynchronous \textbf{C++17} software bridges for real-time trajectory interpolation and dynamics validation.
    \item \textbf{Real Robot Arm Testing \& Calibration:} Having programmed industrial manipulators using \textbf{ABB RAPID} in production facilities and calibrated multi-sensor suites (RGB-D, LiDAR, Encoders, IMU) for autonomous systems, I am deeply familiar with Tool Center Point (TCP) calibration, coordinate frames (TF), and validating execution logic directly on physical hardware.
    \item \textbf{Build, Release, Docker \& Code Quality:} Across both academic and industry roles (b-it bots @Home and Navilan Solutions), I maintain containerized deployment setups via \textbf{Docker}, build automated test suites, enforce rigorous code reviews, and leverage CI/CD pipelines and configuration management to ensure every installation remains reproducible and robust.
    \item \textbf{Hands-on Troubleshooting \& Systems Thinking:} I routinely diagnose hardware-software interfaces and sensor anomalies using \textbf{rosbag, RViz, and Foxglove Studio}, achieving a \textbf{95\% tracking success rate} in closed-loop trajectory execution by methodically isolating timing jitter and kinematic drift.
\end{itemize}
