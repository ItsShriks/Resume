I am writing to express my strong interest in the Robotics Software Engineer (ROS2) position currently open with your Munich-based robotics team, as advertised by Precision Resource Group / iO Advanced Mobility. As a Master's student in Autonomous Systems at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg with a B.Tech in Mechatronics Engineering, I bring hands-on experience in ROS2 software development, physical hardware bring-up, and field troubleshooting. I am eager to contribute to your fast-growing startup and help deploy reliable, real-world robotic systems into customer environments.

\vspace{2pt}

My profile aligns with the key requirements of the role in the following areas:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=2pt, parsep=0pt, topsep=2pt]

    \item \textbf{ROS2 Software Development \& Integration}:\\
    In my work as a Core Member of the b-it bots @Home robotics group, I develop and integrate real-time ROS2 perception, sensor validation, and tracking software packages in modern C++17 and Python. I am proficient in configuring coordinate transforms (\texttt{tf2}), Nav2/MoveIt navigation workflows, and custom telemetry data streams.

    \item \textbf{Hands-on Bring-Up \& Field Troubleshooting}:\\
    During my tenure at Sakar Robotics and SSIG Manufacturing, I led physical hardware bring-up, sensor calibration, and HIL testing. I have a proven track record of troubleshooting complex software-hardware interfaces and execution latency issues under Linux, ensuring robotic components run reliably on-site.

    \item \textbf{Customer Constraints \& Practical Engineering}:\\
    I enjoy hands-on engineering and collaborating directly with end-users. I have experience converting mechanical CAD designs (SolidWorks, Creo) and customer feedback into software specifications, ensuring system maintainability as products transition from prototype to production.

    \item \textbf{Bilingual \& Global Competence}:\\
    I am fluent in English (C2) and German (B2), which allows me to communicate clearly and pragmatically with German and European clients during deployments and on-site troubleshooting.

\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}

I am highly motivated to join your ambitious team in Munich and am available to start immediately, initially as a working student (15--20 hours per week) with the goal of transitioning to full-time employment upon my upcoming graduation.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to the opportunity to discuss how my technical skills and hands-on deployment experience align with your growth plans.
