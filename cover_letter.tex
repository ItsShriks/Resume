\textbf{Re: Application for Robotics Engineer -- Learning-Based Control}

\vspace{5pt}
Dear Hiring Team,

\vspace{5pt}
I would like to apply for the Robotics Engineer role on your learning-based control team in Dadar. The part of your posting about the gap between where a model says to move and where the arm actually goes caught my attention, because my Master's thesis at Hochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg deals with a closely related problem: turning a planner's output into motion that a real controller can follow while the robot is moving. I would like to keep working on that with real robots, and to do it back in Mumbai, where I went to school.

\vspace{4pt}
For the thesis I built a MuJoCo model of the Neobotix MMO-700 (MPO-700 base, UR5e arm, Robotiq 2F-85 gripper) that runs a full autonomous drive, reach, grasp and lift sequence. The control side couples a visibility-aware MPC planner, which uses a reduced-order robot model formulated as a weighted QP, with a high-frequency TSID controller. The ROS~2 bridge and the adapters for trajectory interpolation and execution are written in C++17, and the code is at \href{https://github.com/ItsShriks/porta_ros2_ws}{github.com/ItsShriks/porta\_ros2\_ws}. Earlier I wrote analytical inverse kinematics with KDL for a UR5 and benchmarked collision-free trajectories in MoveIt (\href{https://github.com/ItsShriks/ur5_simulation}{github.com/ItsShriks/ur5\_simulation}).

\vspace{4pt}
Much of my work has also been on real hardware. On a real MMO-700 I wrote the ROS~2 bring-up and hardware interfaces for the base, UR5e arm, Robotiq gripper, LiDARs, RealSense L515 and IMU, and connected its object detection to LLM and vision-language models for semantic object retrieval and task planning. At Sakar Robotics I worked on 7 robot platforms: servo and BLDC motors, drives and gearboxes, LiDAR, encoder and IMU integration, and URDF models built from our CAD assemblies. With the b-it bots RoboCup@Home team I calibrate RGB-D, LiDAR and odometry on the Toyota HSR and debug hardware-software problems in rosbag and Foxglove; my person-following there reaches a \textbf{95\% tracking success rate}.

\vspace{4pt}
You also ask for honest metrics. As a research assistant I trained PointNet++ on pseudo-labels for LiDAR segmentation and got 0.80 mIoU, but I always report that number as preliminary, because it is measured against the pseudo-labels and not real ground truth. The stronger result from that project was registering field annotations to the LiDAR data at 98.8\% inliers. Day to day I work in Python and C++17 on Linux, with Git and Docker.

\vspace{4pt}
I can start full-time and on site in Mumbai from \textbf{February 2027}, after finishing my Master's. I would be happy to walk you through the MuJoCo model or the thesis code in a call.
