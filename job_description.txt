We're an early-stage robotics startup founded out of TUM MIRMI and backed by €1M+ in pre-seed funding from a leading European VC. Our mission is to bring the latest advances in Robot Learning and Physical AI into real industrial deployment.

We're looking for builders: Engineers who enjoy taking things apart, putting them back together, and making complex systems work. You'll work across the entire robotics stack - from mechanics and CAD to sensors, robot programming and industrial deployments - building real automation systems that leave the lab and enter factories.
Aufgaben

Design, build, install and troubleshoot industrial robotic applications and customer deployments
Design applications and mechanical components in CAD
Integrate sensors, cameras, pneumatics, end-effectors and industrial I/Os
Manufacture, assemble, test and iterate prototypes and customer systems
Support in creating drawings and documentation for robotic cells
Qualifikation

Currently pursuing a degree in Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, Automation Engineering or a related field
Hands-on mentality and enthusiasm for building real hardware
Experience with CAD, 3D-printing and manufacturing technolgies
First experience in automation technology, robotics or automation machinery
Independent, pragmatic and solution-oriented way of working
Experience with one or more of the following makes you stand out:
PLC & Industrial communication (Siemens/Codesys, Profinet/EtherCAT, ...)
Robot programming (for example UR, Kuka, Fanuc..)
Pneumatics or electrical systems
Benefits

Take Ownership: Join an early-stage startup where your ideas have a direct impact on the company, product, and technology.

State-of-the-Art Tools and Machinery: Work hands-on with the latest robotics platforms, machine tools, 3D-printers and more.

Accelerate Your Growth: Take on challenging problems, learn how to build turnkey automation solutions from first design to safety certification, and grow faster than in almost any other environment.

Freedom & Responsibility: Shape your role around your strengths and interests with a high degree of ownership and autonomy.

Great Perks: Free dinners, gym membership, team events, and other startup perks.

Grow With Us: Excellent interns and working students have the opportunity to join full-time and help build the company from its earliest days.

Team & Culture: Join a team of young and ambitious builders, who enjoy solving hard problems and have fun playing with the latest technolgies.
We appreciate a builders mindset more than a polished CV. If you're excited about robotics, love building things, and want to work on technology that will shape the future of manufacturing, we'd love to hear from you.
