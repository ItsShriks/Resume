Robotics Software Engineer (ROS2) 
80,000 - 100,000 EUR
Munich based team - (Remote-First + Customer Travel)

A fast-growing robotics team is looking for a Robotics Software Engineer with strong ROS2 experience to help build and deploy real-world robotic systems into customer environments. This opening is for someone who enjoys hands-on engineering and being front-facing - supporting deployments, collaborating directly with end users, and making sure what is built in development performs reliably on-site.


This is also a great fit for someone who wants the pace and ownership of an ambitious startup where the focus is on shipping, deploying, and iterating with real customers. Their ambitious team is already gaining huge traction and is now preparing for its next growth chapter, including an upcoming significant seed raise in the coming months, plus a clear plan to expand into the US market too.

 
What you will be doing
Developing and integrating robotics software using ROS2
Supporting customer deployments (on-site work when required) and troubleshooting in the field
Working closely with customers to understand real constraints and translate them into practical, robust solutions
Improving system performance, stability, and maintainability as products move from prototype to production use
 
What they are looking for
ROS2 experience is essential
Comfortable with customer-facing work (clear communication, pragmatic problem-solving)
Willingness to travel to customer sites as needed
Industry background can be varied: mobile robotics, industrial automation, drones, autonomous systems, etc.
Programming language is flexible (capability matters more than a specific stack)
 

If you are interested and would like to learn more, please apply as soon as possible and I will be in touch shortly to discuss in more detail.
