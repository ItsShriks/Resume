We Could Use Someone Like You in Our Crew.
ModSquad is looking for detail-oriented and culturally aware German and English bilingual professionals to join our Content Annotation team. In this role, you’ll work with a wide range of digital audio content, including music, podcasts, and audiobooks, helping ensure that content and search experiences are accurately understood and represented for audiences around the world.
If German is your primary language and you have a strong understanding of the language, culture, and local content, this is an opportunity to put your language expertise and cultural knowledge to work in a dynamic global project.
What You’ll Do
Review and annotate music, podcasts, audiobooks, and related search scenarios with accuracy and cultural awareness
Use your language expertise and cultural knowledge to understand content, context, and user intent
Listen to and evaluate audio content when needed, considering relevance, context, and local cultural nuances
Research information online to verify details and support accurate evaluations
Apply project guidelines consistently while using good judgment when handling nuanced or subjective content
Clearly document your findings, insights, and questions in English
Complete assigned annotation tasks within established 1–2 week project timelines across rotating project types
Incorporate feedback and escalate questions or issues when additional clarification is needed
Project Commitment
30 hours per week
Hours of Operation (Pacific Time)
5:00 AM to 4:00 PM PST
What We’re Looking For
German as a primary language, with strong written and verbal English proficiency
Strong understanding of the culture, language, and local content
Familiarity with Indonesian music, podcasts, audiobooks, and other digital media
Comfortable exploring and navigating audio streaming platforms on both desktop and mobile devices
Excellent attention to detail and a strong commitment to accuracy and consistency
Experience with content annotation, rating, or evaluation, including making subjective assessments and applying defined criteria consistently
Proficient with Google Workspace, particularly Google Sheets, as well as web-based tools and platforms
Reliable, adaptable, and open to receiving and incorporating feedback
Workspace Requirements
Dedicated laptop or desktop computer running Windows 11 or macOS Tahoe
Willingness to install required ModSquad security systems on your computer and mobile phone
Stable broadband internet connection of 50 Mbps or higher
8 GB RAM or greater
Good quality headset with clear audio
Dual monitors are highly recommended
Please note:  A Chromebook is not sufficient for ModSquad projects.
