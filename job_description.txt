In the AI Department, you'll develop the cognitive abilities of our robots, enabling them to understand and interact with their surroundings. You'll work on algorithms that make machines intelligent and adaptive—ranging from object recognition and language processing to decision-making in complex environments. You'll be part of a team that pushes technological boundaries and collaborates closely with software, hardware, and product management teams. Your Mission & Challenges
As a Teleoperations Intern, you will support the setup, operation, and monitoring of teleoperated robotic systems used for demonstrations, internal projects, testing, and AI data collection.
Your responsibilities may include:
Support the operation of humanoid and dual-arm robotic systems during teleoperation sessions
Assist with robot setup, calibration, and preparation for demos, customer showcases, and internal testing
Help monitor teleoperation workflows and ensure stable robot performance during operation
Collaborate with AI and robotics teams on teleoperation-based data collection activities
Assist with troubleshooting hardware, sensor, or operational issues during robot sessions
Support testing activities involving robotic perception systems, sensors, and manipulation tasks
Help maintain documentation, operational procedures, and testing protocols
Work closely with multidisciplinary teams across AI, robotics, and applications

Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Robotics, Mechanical Engineering, Computer Science, AI, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical field
