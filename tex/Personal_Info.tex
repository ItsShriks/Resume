\section{Summary}
Robotics engineer working on manipulator control, robot modelling and hardware bring-up, currently finishing an M.Sc. in Autonomous Systems in Germany (Feb 2027). Master's thesis: a \textbf{MuJoCo} model of a Neobotix mobile manipulator (UR5e arm, Robotiq gripper) running a full drive--reach--grasp--lift pipeline, with an \textbf{MPC} planner on a reduced-order model (weighted QP) coupled to a \textbf{TSID} controller, in C++17 and ROS 2. Wrote the ROS 2 bring-up for the real MMO-700 and connected its perception to LLM and vision-language models. Before that, two years at Sakar Robotics on \textbf{7 robot platforms} (URDF models, motors, sensors), and perception for the Toyota HSR with a \textbf{95\% tracking success rate}.
