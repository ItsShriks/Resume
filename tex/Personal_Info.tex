\section{Summary}

Master's thesis: a \textbf{MuJoCo} model of a Neobotix mobile manipulator that runs a full drive--reach--grasp--lift pipeline, with a visibility-aware MPC planner coupled to a TSID controller in development. Perception and navigation member of the b-it bots RoboCup@Home team (Toyota HSR, \textbf{95\% person-tracking success}, Help Me Carry task completed at RoboCup German Open 2025). Before the Master's, two years at Sakar Robotics as Scrum Master of a \textbf{10-member team}, working on 7 robot platforms and turning CAD assemblies into simulation-ready URDF models.
